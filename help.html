How to Download & Share Your Assessment Dossier

Learn how to export and share your official 4-page Keerthan Strength Lab assessment PDF via WhatsApp, AirDrop, or Mail without broken link or "Not Found URL" errors.

🍏 iPhone & iPad (iOS) 🤖 Android Devices ⚠️ Why "Not Found URL" Happens ❓ Common Questions (FAQ)
⚠️ Why does iPhone give "Not Found URL" when sharing?

When an iPhone user taps the Safari Share button while viewing a generated PDF, Safari mistakenly shares the temporary internal memory link (starting with blob:https://...) instead of the actual document file. Blob links only exist on that specific phone in memory — when your coach or friend clicks that link on their phone, it displays "URL Not Found" / 404.

The Solution: Always use the portal's built-in 📤 Share PDF button or share the physical file from your iPhone's Files app.

🍏

iPhone & iPad (iOS) Step-by-Step Guide

Recommended Method 1

Tap "Share PDF" (One-Touch WhatsApp)

  • 1 Fill in your details and draw or type your signature on the form.
  • 2 Tap the gold 📤 Share PDF button in the submit bar or header.
  • 3 The native iOS Share Sheet will slide up showing the real .pdf document attached.
  • 4 Tap WhatsApp, AirDrop, Messages, or Mail.
✅ Result: Your recipient receives the physical PDF document in their chat. No broken web links!
Method 2

Share Saved PDF via iPhone "Files" App

  • 1 Tap 📥 Download PDF on the portal to save the file.
  • 2 Open the built-in Files app 📁 (blue folder icon) on your iPhone.
  • 3 Go to Browse ➔ Downloads (or On My iPhone > Downloads).
  • 4 Tap and hold (long press) the Keerthan_Strength_Lab_Assessment...pdf file.
  • 5 Tap Share in the popup menu ➔ select WhatsApp or AirDrop.
💡 Pro-Tip: The Files app always sends the complete 4-page vector PDF with your signature permanently locked.
Method 3

Safari Browser "Send as PDF" Setting

  • 1 If you downloaded and opened the PDF in Safari, tap the Safari Share icon (square with arrow up).
  • 2 At the top of the Share Sheet below the title, tap Options.
  • 3 Under Send As, select PDF (do NOT leave it on "Automatic").
  • 4 Tap Done, then select WhatsApp or Mail.
🛡️ Why this works: Forcing "PDF" prevents Safari from sending an invalid web link to the recipient.
🤖

Android Devices Step-by-Step Guide

Recommended Method 1

Tap "Share PDF" (Direct App Share)

  • 1 Complete all fields on the assessment form and sign.
  • 2 Tap 📤 Share PDF.
  • 3 The Android System Share Sheet appears.
  • 4 Select WhatsApp, Google Drive, or Gmail.
✅ Instant Delivery: Android attaches the complete PDF document directly to your selected chat or email.
Method 2

Direct Download & Files Storage

  • 1 Tap 📥 Generate & Download Official PDF.
  • 2 The PDF downloads immediately to your phone's storage.
  • 3 Swipe down your top notification bar to tap the completed download, OR open Files by Google / My Files ➔ Downloads.
  • 4 Tap the Share icon next to the file to send it to anyone.
💡 Chrome Note: If Google Chrome displays "File might be harmful... download anyway?", tap Download anyway. It is 100% safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

📄 Does the downloaded PDF contain all 4 pages and my signature?

Yes, 100%. The system compiles all 4 official Keerthan Strength Lab assessment pages, including Client Screening, PAR-Q questions, read-only trainer metrics, and your legal signature. The document is permanently flattened into vector text so nothing is lost.

🖨️ How can I print the PDF from my mobile device?

Tap the 📤 Share PDF button (or open the saved PDF in your Files app), scroll down the share menu options, and tap Print. On iPhone, this uses AirPrint; on Android, it connects to your WiFi or default cloud printer.

🔒 Is my assessment data stored on any external server?

No. The document generation runs client-side in your own browser using secure vector PDF compilation. Your health data, PAR-Q answers, and signature remain private on your device.
